How custom orders work

From your idea to in your hands.

Got something in mind that isn’t in the shop? Cake toppers, drink stirrers, signs and more, printed just for you. Here’s how it works.

  1. 01

    Tell us your idea

    What you’d like made, any names or words, the occasion, your colour and the size you need. A sketch or a photo you like helps too.

  2. 02

    We send a preview

    You get a digital preview of the design.

  3. 03

    We print it

    Once you’re happy, we print it in PLA.

  4. 04

    Post or collect

    We post it out carefully packed, or you can collect. confirm delivery options

Good to know

  • PLA PlasticOur cake toppers are made from 3D printed PLA, a plant based plastic made from renewable resources like corn starch and sugarcane.
  • Single useToppers are single use only. 3D printed layer lines cannot be fully cleaned after contact with food. Keep as a memento or disposal.
  • Keep away from heatPLA softens with heat — hand wash only with cool soapy water.
  • Keep away from small childrenCan contain small parts and pointed sticks.
